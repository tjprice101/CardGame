# Generic Card Abilities

*A collection of universal card effects that can be applied to any deck, using standardized game terminology.*

---

## Ophanim Abilities (Universal Spells)

### Draw & Card Selection
1. **Archive Delve** — Draw 2 cards
2. **Deep Search** — Look at the top 4 cards, take 1 card, and put the rest on the bottom
3. **Careful Study** — Look at the top 3 cards, take 1 card, discard 1 card, and put the rest on the bottom
4. **Cascading Vision** — Draw 1 card for each active Cherubim on board (max 2)
5. **Seraphic Revelation** — If you control 1+ active Seraphim, Draw 2 cards
6. **Empty Draw** — If no Seraphim are on board, Draw 3 cards
7. **Measured Wisdom** — If hand size is 3 or less, Draw 2 cards
8. **Abundant Wisdom** — If hand size is 6 or more, Draw 1 card and +40 Oblivion

### Deck Manipulation
9. **Cycle Rite** — Choose and discard 1 card, Draw 2 cards
10. **Deep Cycle** — Choose and discard 2 cards, Draw 3 cards
11. **Deck Renewal** — Shuffle discard into deck, Draw 1 card
12. **Grave Return** — Shuffle discard into deck
13. **Precision Recall** — Salvage any 1 Ophanim
14. **Resource Recall** — Salvage any 1 Cherubim
15. **Champion Recall** — Salvage any 1 Seraphim
16. **Curated Salvage** — Look at the top 3 cards in discard, Salvage 1 of them

### Tutors & Search
17. **Ophanim Search** — Search your deck for 1 matching Ophanim
18. **Cherubim Search** — Search your deck for 1 matching Cherubim  
19. **Seraphim Search** — Search your deck for 1 matching Seraphim
20. **Flexible Tutor** — Search your deck for 1 card matching Ophanim or Cherubim
21. **Champion Tutor** — Search your deck for 1 card matching Seraphim or Angel
22. **Conditional Tutor** — If you have played 2+ cards this turn, Search your deck for any 1 card

### Direct Oblivion (Small)
23. **Minor Strike** — +40 Oblivion
24. **Modest Strike** — +60 Oblivion
25. **Solid Strike** — +80 Oblivion
26. **Trade Offering** — Choose and discard 1 card, +100 Oblivion
27. **Heavy Trade** — Choose and discard 2 cards, +160 Oblivion

### Direct Oblivion (Medium)

30. **Heavy Barrage** — +180 Oblivion
31. **Empowered Barrage** — If you control 1+ active Seraphim, +200 Oblivion

### Direct Oblivion (Large)
33. **Major Assault** — +240 Oblivion
34. **Critical Assault** — +300 Oblivion
35. **Apocalyptic Strike** — +400 Oblivion
36. **Finisher** — +500 Oblivion, Choose and discard 1 card

### Conditional Oblivion Boosts

### Hybrid Effects

## Seraphim Abilities (Front-Line Units)

### On-Play Effects
1. **Aggressive Summon** — On play: +80 Oblivion, Draw 1 card
2. **Tactical Summon** — On play: +60 Oblivion, Look at the top 2 cards and take 1 card
3. **Cycling Summon** — On play: Choose and discard 1 card, Draw 2 cards
4. **Salvage Summon** — On play: +50 Oblivion, Salvage any 1 Ophanim
5. **Search Summon** — On play: Search your deck for 1 matching Ophanim
6. **Renewal Summon** — On play: +70 Oblivion, Shuffle discard into deck
7. **Empowering Summon** — On play: Seraphim bonuses are amplified by +15
8. **Support Summon** — On play: Adjacent active Cherubim gain +1 durability

### While Active - Card Play Bonuses
9. **Steady Presence** — While active: +15 Oblivion per card played
10. **Strong Presence** — While active: +25 Oblivion per card played
11. **Dominant Presence** — While active: +40 Oblivion per card played
12. **Spell Focus** — While active: +20 Oblivion whenever you play an Ophanim while active
13. **Support Focus** — While active: +15 Oblivion whenever you summon a Cherubim while active
14. **Draw Engine** — While active: Whenever you play an Ophanim, Draw 1 card (once per turn)

### While Active - Amplifiers
15. **Spell Amplifier** — While active: Ophanim Oblivion effects are increased by +10%
16. **Support Amplifier** — While active: Cherubim on-summon Oblivion effects are increased by +20
17. **Adjacent Synergy** — While active: Adjacent active Seraphim gain +20 Oblivion per card played
18. **Board Synergy** — While active: All active Seraphim gain +10 Oblivion per card played

### While Active - Resource Effects
19. **Draw Support** — While active: Your first card each turn draws 1 extra card
20. **Search Support** — While active: Once per turn, Search effects find +1 additional valid option before choosing
21. **Salvage Support** — While active: Once per turn, Salvage effects can retrieve 1 additional card
22. **Discard Mitigation** — While active: Your first discard each turn is prevented

### Triggered Abilities
23. **Chain Trigger** — While active: After you play your 3rd card each turn, +100 Oblivion
24. **Spell Trigger** — While active: After you play your 2nd Ophanim each turn, Draw 1 card
25. **Unit Trigger** — While active: When another unit leaves play, +40 Oblivion

## Design Notes

These abilities are intentionally generic and avoid set-specific mechanics (Heat, Radiance, Patience, Strain, etc.) to ensure they can be adapted to any deck archetype. When implementing these in actual cards:

- Adjust Oblivion values based on card rarity and cost
- Consider durability values for Seraphim and Cherubim
- Add set-specific flavor and mechanics as appropriate
- Balance trigger conditions with power level
- Ensure effects synergize with the intended deck strategy
