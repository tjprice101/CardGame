Card Abilities (Generic)

Ophanim Abilities (50)
1. Tithe of Dust: On-play: gain 3 Oblivion and discard 1 card.
2. Hollow Offering: On-play: discard your hand, then gain Oblivion equal to discarded cards +2.
3. Quiet Subsidy: On-play: your next card costs 1 less resource.
4. Selfless Rupture: On-play: remove 1 allied front-slot unit and gain Oblivion equal to its level +2.
5. Mercy Ledger: On-play: if hand size is 3 or less, gain 2 Oblivion and draw 2.
6. Ashen Revelation: On-play: draw 2. If no Seraphim are on board, draw 1 more.
7. Seventh Seal: On-play: look at the top 5 cards, keep 2, place the rest on bottom.
8. Cascading Descent: On-play: draw 1 for each allied Cherubim on board.
9. Whisper Archive: On-play: search deck for an Ophanim and add it to hand.
10. Echo Chamber: On-play: repeat the previous Ophanim effect used this turn (cannot copy Echo Chamber).
11. Mandate Ward: On-play: choose an allied Seraphim; it gains ward for your next card play.
12. Severance Rite: On-play: discard 1 card, then remove 1 allied front-slot unit and draw 2.
13. Weight of Sins: On-play: choose 1 allied unit; clear its temporary buffs and gain 6 Oblivion.
14. Celestial Embargo: On-play: you cannot play another Ophanim this turn; draw 2.
15. Binding Light: On-play: if you control 2+ Seraphim, your next attack gains +25 base Oblivion.
16. Call to the Meek: On-play: replace an allied Seraphim with an Angel from your Extra Deck; that Angel gains +1 bonus power.
17. Ascension Protocol: On-play: if you control an Angel, refresh 1 of its actions.
18. Wings of Expenditure: On-play: sacrifice an allied Angel; draw 3 and gain 2 Oblivion.
19. Unnamed Host: On-play: if no Seraphim are on board, summon a temporary 2-power Angel token.
20. Intercessory Rite: On-play: return an Angel from discard to your Extra Deck and reduce its next summon requirement by 1.
21. Oblivion Tithe: On-play: gain 5 Oblivion, then discard 2 cards.
22. Blinding Choir: On-play: draw 3, then if hand size exceeds 7, discard down to 7.
23. Torn Firmament: On-play: discard 1 random card, then draw 2.
24. Dead Reckoning: On-play: reveal top 3 cards of your deck; banish 1, add 1 to hand, discard 1.
25. Last Rite: On-play: if deck has 1 card left, draw it and gain 5 Oblivion.
26. Spiral Tax: On-play: your next card gains +15 base Oblivion and costs +1 resource.
27. Suture Loop: On-play: place this card on bottom of deck and draw 1.
28. Fading Mirror: On-play: repeat this card's first effect at 50% value.
29. Grave Appointment: On-play: choose a card in discard; it costs 1 less when next played.
30. Open Palm: On-play: draw 1 and gain +6 Oblivion.
31. Closed Fist: On-play: discard 1 and gain +16 Oblivion.
32. Split Verdict: On-play: choose one: draw 2, or gain 4 Oblivion.
33. Sacrament of Numbers: On-play: gain +5 Oblivion for each different allied card type on board.
34. Empty Chapel: On-play: if no back-slot units are on board, summon a 1-durability Cherubim token.
35. Pilgrim Search: On-play: look at top 3 cards, place 1 in discard, draw 1.
36. Red Letter: On-play: your next attack gains +20 base Oblivion.
37. Pale Letter: On-play: your next card draw effect draws 1 extra card.
38. Dust Covenant: On-play: your next discard effect is prevented.
39. Stolen Dawn: On-play: skip your next draw action and draw 3 now.
40. Rescript: On-play: choose an allied card; reset one per-turn limit on it.
41. Ash Lottery: On-play: reveal top card; if Ophanim draw it, otherwise gain +10 Oblivion.
42. Sanctified Recall: On-play: put 2 cards from discard on top of deck in any order.
43. Narrow Path: On-play: if hand size is exactly 2, gain +30 Oblivion.
44. Broad Path: On-play: if hand size is 6 or more, draw 1 and gain +8 Oblivion.
45. Consecrated Delay: On-play: add +1 cooldown step to your next attack, then gain +25 Oblivion.
46. Soft Collapse: On-play: remove 1 allied temporary shield or ward effect and draw 2.
47. Borrowed Breath: On-play: if this is your third Ophanim this turn, draw 2.
48. Minor Apocalypse: On-play: discard 2 random cards from hand, then draw 2.
49. Seal of Passage: On-play: your next summoned unit enters with +1 durability.
50. Final Catalog: On-play: if discard has 12+ cards, gain 6 Oblivion and draw 1.

Cherubim Abilities (50)
1. Shrine Fuse: On-summon: gain 1 Oblivion. On-board: whenever you discard a card, gain 1 Oblivion.
2. Grinding Choir: On-summon: draw 1. On-board: whenever you discard, gain +1 card draw progress.
3. Tithe Engine: On-summon: discard 1, gain 2 Oblivion. On-board: once per card play, gain 1 Oblivion when hand size is 3 or less.
4. Wellspring Frame: On-summon: gain 2 Oblivion. On-board: whenever a front-slot unit leaves play, gain 1 Oblivion.
5. Hollow Sanctum: On-summon: if no Seraphim are on board, gain 3 Oblivion. On-board: while no Seraphim are on board, Ophanim gain +4 Oblivion.
6. Archive Warden: On-summon: look at top 2 cards, keep 1. On-board: whenever you play an Ophanim, scry 1.
7. Lectern Core: On-summon: draw 1. On-board: if a card play had no draw effect, gain 1 Oblivion.
8. Echo Choir: On-summon: draw 1. On-board: when a single effect draws 2+, draw 1 extra.
9. Undying Record: On-summon: shuffle up to 3 discard cards into deck. On-board: when deck is empty, gain 3 Oblivion instead of drawing.
10. Lantern Vault: On-summon: draw 1. On-board: hand size cap is +2.
11. Bulwark Sigil: On-summon: reduce next Seraphim cost by 1. On-board: Seraphim you play cost 1 less resource.
12. Resonant Throne: On-summon: choose a Seraphim, it gains +1 bonus power. On-board: highest-level Seraphim gains +1 bonus power.
13. Pyre Ascent: On-summon: gain 1 Oblivion for each Seraphim on board. On-board: when a Seraphim leaves play, next Angel summon requirement is reduced by 1.
14. Eternal Formation: On-summon: grant allied Seraphim stabilization. On-board: allied Seraphim cannot be repositioned by your own effects.
15. Mending Choir: On-summon: restore 1 durability to an allied unit. On-board: when you play a Cherubim, restore 1 durability to another allied Cherubim.
16. Angelic Conscription: On-summon: reduce next Angel summon requirement by 1. On-board: Angels in Extra Deck require 1 less summon material.
17. Lower Heaven: On-summon: choose an Angel, it counts as Seraphim this sequence. On-board: Angels count as Seraphim for your effects.
18. Martyr Ground: On-summon: draw 1. On-board: when an allied Angel leaves play, draw 1.
19. Chorus Meek: On-summon: if 2+ Angels are controlled, they gain +1 bonus power. On-board: Angels keep +1 bonus power while this remains.
20. Intercessor Gate: On-summon: swap 1 Angel from board with one in Extra Deck. On-board: once per sequence, Angel swap is free.
21. Static Veil: On-summon: your next Ophanim costs +1 resource and gains +20 Oblivion. On-board: Ophanim cost +1 and gain +8 Oblivion.
22. Oppressive Seal: On-summon: lock Ophanim count to 1 for current sequence. On-board: only 1 Ophanim can be played each sequence.
23. Blind Archive: On-summon: draw 1, then discard 1. On-board: your first deck search each sequence finds +1 additional valid card before choosing.
24. Crumbling Mandate: On-summon: disable 1 allied Cherubim to gain 3 Oblivion. On-board: when a Cherubim is disabled, gain 2 Oblivion.
25. Obsidian Covenant: On-summon: gain 4 Oblivion. On-board: direct card-effect Oblivion gains are reduced by 1 (minimum 0).
26. Pilgrim Bell: On-summon: gain 2 Oblivion. On-board: your first card each sequence gains +5 Oblivion.
27. Gilded Census: On-summon: draw 1. On-board: every third card played draws 1.
28. Pale Census: On-summon: if hand size is 2 or less, draw 1. On-board: while hand size is 2 or less, cards gain +3 Oblivion.
29. Narrow Gate: On-summon: if exactly one back slot is occupied, gain 4 Oblivion. On-board: lone Cherubim trigger value +1.
30. Open Gate: On-summon: if both back slots are occupied, draw 1. On-board: first Cherubim trigger each sequence repeats.
31. Dust Altar: On-summon: sacrifice 1 card from hand to draw 2. On-board: discard effects draw +1 once each sequence.
32. Quiet Novitiate: On-summon: prevent your next discard. On-board: first discard each sequence is prevented.
33. Ledger Warden: On-summon: gain 2 Oblivion. On-board: whenever you gain Oblivion, gain +2 more (cap +10 per sequence).
34. Burden Warden: On-summon: lose 2 cards from hand, gain 8 Oblivion. On-board: whenever you draw 2+ from one effect, lose 2 Oblivion then draw 1.
35. Foster Chapel: On-summon: next summoned unit gains +1 durability. On-board: summoned units enter with +1 durability.
36. Hollow Bell: On-summon: gain 3 Oblivion. On-board: whenever an allied unit leaves play, gain +8 Oblivion.
37. Grave Tutor: On-summon: return 1 discard card to hand. On-board: after discarding 2 cards in a sequence, return 1 random discard card to hand.
38. Last Candle: On-summon: if deck is empty, gain 3 Oblivion. On-board: empty-deck draws convert to 3 Oblivion.
39. First Candle: On-summon: draw 1. On-board: your first played card each sequence draws 1.
40. Ordinance Clerk: On-summon: next attack cooldown increase is reduced by 1. On-board: first attack each sequence gets -1 cooldown increase.
41. Mausoleum Scribe: On-summon: gain 1 Oblivion. On-board: every fourth card played draws 1.
42. Refuge Choir: On-summon: if no Angels are controlled, Seraphim gain +1 bonus power. On-board: while no Angels are controlled, Seraphim keep +1 bonus power.
43. Throne Choir: On-summon: if an Angel is controlled, gain 6 Oblivion. On-board: while an Angel is controlled, Ophanim gain +10 Oblivion on-play.
44. Ash Interdict: On-summon: set minimum card cost to 1 for this sequence. On-board: cost reductions cannot move below 1.
45. Relic Broker: On-summon: banish 1 discard card to gain 2 Oblivion. On-board: once per sequence, banish a discard card to gain 2 Oblivion.
46. Lantern Broker: On-summon: banish 1 hand card to draw 2. On-board: first hand-banish each sequence draws +1.
47. Iron Hymn: On-summon: mark 1 front-slot unit. On-board: first marked unit destroyed returns with 1 durability.
48. Velvet Hymn: On-summon: your next card played gains +8 Oblivion. On-board: first card played each sequence gains +5 Oblivion.
49. Quiet Tribunal: On-summon: if 3+ cards are played this sequence, discard 1 then draw 1. On-board: after the third card play each sequence, apply discard 1 then draw 1.
50. Final Sanctuary: On-summon: draw 2. On-board: when this Cherubim leaves play, gain +12 Oblivion.
