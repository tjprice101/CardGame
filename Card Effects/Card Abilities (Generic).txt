## Design Notes

This is an **ability bank** for modular card design, not individual card specifications. Effects are intentionally generic and avoid set-specific mechanics (Heat, Radiance, Patience, Strain, etc.) to ensure they can be adapted to any deck archetype.

**Total Effects:** 130 Ophanim abilities + 95 Seraphim abilities + 55 Angel abilities + 130 Cherubim abilities = 410 generic effects

### Core Mechanics
- **Ophanim**: One-time spell effects (play and resolve)
- **Seraphim**: Front-line units with attack capabilities and attack cooldowns. Persist until forced to depart through specific card effects
- **Angel**: Elite Extra Deck units with powerful effects, summoned through special requirements. Persist until forced to depart
- **Cherubim**: Back-line support units with durability (loses 1 per card played). ONLY Cherubim have durability
- **Heavenly-Bond**: Special category of effects that dynamically respond to board state, creating adaptive gameplay moments

### Implementation Guidelines
- Mix and match effects to create unique cards
- Adjust Oblivion values based on card rarity, cost, and overall power budget
- Scale Cherubim durability values appropriately (typically 3-6 for common/rare, higher for legendary)
- Add set-specific mechanics and flavor on top of these base templates
- Balance trigger conditions with power level
- Combine multiple effects for higher-rarity cards

### Effect Categories Explained
- **On-summon/On-play**: Immediate one-time effects when card enters play
- **While on board/While active**: Continuous effects while unit remains in play
- **After X cards played**: Milestone triggers (durability naturally limits how many can occur)
- **On departure**: Effects when unit leaves play (0 durability or removed)
- **Conditional**: Effects that vary based on game state (hand size, board state, etc.)
- **Adjacent**: Effects that interact with units in neighboring slots
- **Heavenly-Bond**: Dynamic effects that check and respond to specific board configurations, creating branching effects based on unit composition, positioning, and state changes

### Using This Bank
1. Choose a card type (Ophanim/Seraphim/Angel/Cherubim)
2. Select 1-4 effects from relevant categories (more effects for higher rarity cards)
3. Adjust values for your set's power level
4. Add set-specific mechanics and resource systems (replace <resource_name> placeholders)
5. Reference specific cards by replacing <card_name> placeholders with actual card names
6. Add thematic flavor and card name
7. For Angels, ensure summoning requirements match the power level of selected abilities
