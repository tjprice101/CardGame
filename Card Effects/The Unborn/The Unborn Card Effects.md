# The Unborn — Card Effects

> **Set**: The Unborn · **Element**: `TheUnborn`
> **Visual Identity**: No unified color palette. Every card has a unique color scheme inherited from the source universe it originated from — no two cards look alike. The glass-shard aesthetic is consistent (fractured planes, impossible refraction, shattered edges) but the color of each shard varies wildly. The Unborn Monarch is the single exception: perfectly still, surrounded by orbiting glass fragments in every color, the only calm point in perpetual collision.
>
> **Core Mechanic — Fracture Depth**: Fracture Depth (integer counter) increases automatically by 1 with every card played — no cards needed to trigger it, it is inevitable. At Depths **4, 8, and 12**, a **Collision Event** fires automatically: burst Oblivion scales with current Depth. Cards reference Depth for conditional effects ("If Fracture Depth ≥ 6..."). The **Tessered** faction uses cards that accelerate Fracture Depth (skipping ahead to thresholds faster). The **Sealed Corpus** faction uses cards that spend Fracture Depth for controlled, immediate Oblivion (sacrificing future threshold power for present efficiency).
>
> **Eternal/Infinite Ancillary — Shard Echo**: When a Collision Event fires (at Depth 4, 8, or 12), gain 1 Shard Echo. Shard Echoes are permanent this-match stacks that multiply the Oblivion of every future Collision Event. Each echo represents a previous collision resonating through every subsequent one — cascading.
>
> **Factions**: Tessered (shatter and scatter across timelines; hit from everywhere at once) · Sealed Corpus (reinforce with temporal paradox; sacrifice memory/Depth for stability and precision). Both factions are equally represented. The **Unborn Monarch** observes all of it from the center of the Glass Between — perfectly still, carrying twelve failed universes inside them, reaching out occasionally to touch one particular fragment as if selecting from a collection.
>
> **Lore**: When universes collide like panes of glass, the fragments of each shattered reality can become conscious. The Unborn are these fragments — beings assembled from the wreckage of universes that never got to finish forming. Each carries ghost-physics from their source universe, which sometimes manifests as wild power surges where their origin's laws briefly overwrite local reality. They did not choose to exist. They were forced into being by the pressure of competing realities leaving no space for nothing. They are the Glass Between given desperate form.

## Mechanic Summary

| Resource | How Generated | How Spent |
|----------|---------------|-----------|
| **Fracture Depth** | Automatic: +1 per card played always; Tessered cards add extra; some Cherubim add extra | Collision Events at 4/8/12 (auto-trigger, Oblivion burst) · Sealed Corpus cards Spend Depth for immediate Oblivion |
| **Shard Echo** *(Eternal/Infinite only)* | +1 each time a Collision Event fires (at 4, 8, 12); some Angels grant additional | Passive amplifier — each stack multiplies Collision Event Oblivion |

**Collision Event Formula**: Oblivion = Fracture Depth × base coefficient, modified by Cherubim passives and Shard Echo multiplier

**Tessered vs Sealed Design Tension**:
- Tessered cards rush Depth, want Collision Events to fire multiple times
- Sealed Corpus cards spend Depth early for controlled bursts, then want to rebuild for threshold events
- Both strategies are valid and stack differently with Cherubim

**Full Fire Gate (Eternal/Infinite)**: Active Seraphim present + Fracture Depth ≥ 10 + Shard Echo ≥ 2
