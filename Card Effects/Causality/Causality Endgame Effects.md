# Causality Endgame Effects

This file summarizes implemented Causality endgame content. It is separate from `Causality Ability Drafts.md`, whose three abilities remain design-only.

## Eternity's Wake Rewards

- **The First Cause Unwritten** — Eternal Light. Soph placement generates 4 Limitless Cosmos and 650 base Divine Light. Uses high-base Ain and Soph attacks.
- **The Last Horizon Remembered** — Eternal Light. Soph placement converts 3 Limitless Light Stacks into 6 Cosmos when prepared.
- **Sovereign Ink of the Black Sun** — persistent Eternal Dark. Consume 2 Cosmos to draw 3 and gain 8 Limitless Light Stacks; returns to hand on a four-card cooldown.
- **Chromatic Verdict of Elsewhen** — Eternal Dark. Consume 3 Cosmos for 7,500 base Divine Light and shuffle discard into the deck.
- **Pearlescent Engine Beyond Sequence** — Eternal Ain Soph Aur. Requires The First Cause Unwritten and Sovereign Ink of the Black Sun, generates 5 Cosmos on summon, and has a 5,200-base Bridge.

## Infinite Cards

- **Origin Script of Every Tomorrow** — Infinite Light; 8 Cosmos plus two cards on Soph placement and major two-attack scaling.
- **Chromatic Horizon Without End** — Infinite Light; converts 4 Light into 9 Cosmos and carries extreme attack scaling.
- **Law-Eater of the Pearl Void** — persistent Infinite Dark; consumes 4 Cosmos for 14,000 base Divine Light, three cards, and 10 Light Stacks.
- **Archive Reborn in Chromatic Ink** — persistent Infinite Dark; consumes 3 Cosmos to search all three card families, shuffle discard, and regenerate 5 Cosmos.
- **Heart Beyond All Causality** — Infinite Ain Soph Aur; generates 10 Cosmos, 4,000 base Divine Light, and two cards on summon, then attacks with a 15,000-base Bridge.

Every Infinite recipe uses only the five Causality Eternal rewards and materials from Rift of Causality.
