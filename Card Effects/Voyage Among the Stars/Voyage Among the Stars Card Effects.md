# Voyage Among the Stars — Card Effects

> **Set**: Voyage Among the Stars · **Element**: `VoyageAmongTheStars`
> **Visual Identity**: Prismatic oil-slick iridescence on pure void black. Each card is a distinct vessel-being — impossible geometry, crystallized gravity, alien doctrine given hull-form.
>
> **Core Mechanic — Void Wake**: Playing cards generates Void Wake (integer counter). Seraphim attacks scale with Wake. Discharge effects cash out all Wake for burst Oblivion. There is no decay — Wake simply waits.
>
> **Eternal/Infinite Ancillary — Trajectory**: When a Discharge occurs (from a card effect or Seraphim attack), gain Trajectory equal to the Wake discharged. Trajectory amplifies the *next* Discharge's Oblivion coefficient. Represents navigational mastery: every journey makes the next more devastating.
>
> **Factions**: Null-Keeled (collapse all back to nothing) · Prismatic Drift (stretch reality to pure experience) · Ironbound Meridian (freeze reality at peak complexity) · Wandering Unbottled (the search itself is the point). All four are present in the base set. **Harrowkeel the Unanchored** is hunted by all four for knowing the Paradox — that all four Doctrines are simultaneously correct.
>
> **Lore**: The Voidfaring are not pilots aboard ships. They ARE ships. Ancient clusters of collapsed stellar material that achieved self-awareness through gravitational recursion, they fold their consciousness into hull-forms as a declaration of self — the vessel is the mind. They wage Doctrinal Wars across the Void Corridor not for territory, but for cosmological dominance. Whoever's Doctrine survives the Corridor's collapse becomes the foundational logic of the next universe.

## Mechanic Summary

| Resource | How Generated | How Spent |
|----------|---------------|-----------|
| **Void Wake** | Cards played (base +1 or more per card; Cherubim/Seraphim passives add more) | Discharge effects (burst Oblivion) · Seraphim synergized attacks (Wake × coefficient) |
| **Trajectory** *(Eternal/Infinite only)* | Gained when Wake is Discharged (= Wake amount discharged) | Amplifies coefficient of the next Discharge event |

**Full Fire Gate (Eternal/Infinite)**: Active Seraphim present + Void Wake ≥ 8 + Trajectory ≥ 1
