## Generation Notes

- If Suno tries to insert real words, regenerate with "no words, no language, vocalizations only" prepended to the Style field.
- If tempo drifts off 165 BPM, regenerate — do not time-stretch (it kills the choir).
- For the boss theme, you can split phases into separate generations using just one phase's tags at a time, then crossfade in-engine.
- Tracks 1, 2, 4 are designed to loop — fade the final 2s and crossfade at the loop point in-engine.
- Track 6 is designed to loop — keep the energy even, no dramatic dips or peaks within the loop.
- Track 7: do NOT let Suno add a triumphant major-key lift. If it does, prepend "no major resolution, stay Phrygian minor throughout, no bright choir ahh moment" to the Style field.
- Track 8: if Suno adds a soft intro, prepend "no soft opening, full intensity from beat one, no dynamic dip anywhere" to the Style field.
