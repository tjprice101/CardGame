# Heavenly Light Set — Midjourney Art Prompts (v3 — Humanoid / Cinematic Anime)

**Element Identity:** Divine radiance, golden light, celestial hierarchy, solar power, Radiance energy.
**Visual Direction:** Humanoid characters whose body and armor designs are deeply integrated with the Heavenly Light theme. Golden plate armor engraved with solar runes and sun-disc motifs. Skin infused with radiance — glowing veins of golden light. Hair made of golden flame or cascading amber radiance. Wings of woven light solid at the base and dissolving into pure light at the tips. Every card is a dynamic cinematic action shot — dramatic angles, spectacular energy effects, epic composition.
**Palette system:** The set uses a warm solar palette with distinct per-card variations — **dawn rose-gold and soft amber** (early morning cards), **deep regal burnished gold and royal amber** (authority cards), **blazing vivid gold and white-hot solar white** (power cards), **electric amber and deep orange-gold** (fury/fire cards), **pale gold and cream-white** (grace/clarity cards), **mirror-polish gold and tawny amber** (titan/beast cards). Each card specifies its exact palette.
**Style tag used on every prompt:** `anime art style, vibrant saturated colors, dynamic cinematic action shot, detailed anime character design, dramatic lighting, spectacular energy effects, epic composition --ar 2:3 --s 850 --q 2`
