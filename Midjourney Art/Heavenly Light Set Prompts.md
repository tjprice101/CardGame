# Heavenly Light Set - Midjourney Art Prompts (v5 - Emberthorn Flame / Painterly Anime)

**Element Identity:** Divine radiance reborn as holy flame, red and yellow fire, silver thorn sanctity, celestial hierarchy, Radiance energy.
**Visual Direction:** Heavenly Light trading card illustration in the niji 6 anime painterly style. Humanoid seraphim, angels, and divine warriors forged from martyr-fire instead of sunlight: ember-red armor cores, furnace-yellow flame hair, white-hot radiance, and polished silver thorn filigree layered through halos, wings, weapons, cathedrals, and living armor. Warm flame palette with per-card variations kept intact: ember crimson and saffron yellow, arterial red and furnace marigold, white-hot yellow and inferno scarlet, ash-silver and ember orange, blackened crimson and molten yellow. Bold ink outlines thick to thin, cel shaded with visible brushwork, painterly and expressive. Figures fill 80% of the frame. Backgrounds are loose expressive luminous brushwork: flame cloud oceans, silver-thorn cathedrals, ember citadels, inferno forges, thorn observatories, burning throne halls, and heavenly battlefields of holy fire. Energy effects are gestural painted flares of red, yellow, white-hot fire, and silver thorn sparks. YGO secret rare card art meets pokemon illustration rare full art composition.
