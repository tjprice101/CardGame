# Eternity's Wake — Boss Art Prompts

**Visual Identity:** Every prompt below opens and closes with the identical style-lock phrasing so Midjourney renders the entire boss roster in one consistent look: strict grayscale base (obsidian black, bone white, neutral gray) with the single explicit exception of prismatic rainbow caustic light confined only to glass and crystal surfaces. Flat cel-shaded anime line art, thick-to-thin ink outlines, painterly brushwork, YGO/Pokemon full-art composition. Only the subject, pose, specific details, and background differ between bosses. The negative list blocks warm/skin-tone drift so the rainbow stays confined to crystal refraction instead of bleeding across the whole image.
**Parameters on every prompt:** `--ar 16:9 --niji 6 --stylize 900 --no skin-tone color, warm color, red skin, orange skin, yellow skin, green tint, blue tint, purple tint, pink tint, brown, tan, gold, bronze, sepia, flat rainbow background wash, multicolor background`
