# Snowbound Voltage Set - Midjourney Art Prompts

**Element Identity:** Snowbound Voltage is frozen circuitry, winter-static divinity, and aurora-blue current trapped in whiteout metal. Sacred machine angels reforged in a polar storm: conductive frost veining through chrome plate, luminous ice filaments wound around reactor cores, voltage arcs as cold and precise as glacier mathematics, and cathedral geometry buried in perpetual blizzard. The set marries Mechanical Dreams' overclock liturgy with an absolute winter  Estrain becomes ice pressure, radiance becomes aurora current, sequence growth becomes a widening storm front.
**Visual Direction:** Snowbound Voltage trading card illustration in the niji 6 anime painterly style. Palette of glacier white, chrome silver, arctic cyan, electric teal, storm-slate black, and pale aurora gold. Frozen antenna arrays, whiteout reactor vaults, polar rail sanctuaries, blizzard cathedral catwalks, and ice-core engine chambers. Mechanical silhouettes coated in permafrost, with voltage discharge trails and drifting snow creating expressive motion. Bold ink outlines thick to thin, cel shaded with visible brushwork, painterly and expressive. Figures fill 80% of the frame. Backgrounds are loose expressive brushwork with blizzard haze, static sparks, and frozen machinery. YGO secret rare card art meets pokemon illustration rare full art composition.
**Parameters on every prompt:** `--ar 2:3 --niji 6 --stylize 900`
