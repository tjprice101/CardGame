# Age of the Butterfly Set - Midjourney Art Prompts

**Story Premise:** Soleth Veth is a world of atmospheric meadows with no fixed up or down, where butterflies define reality through wing-material, resonance, refraction, flame, suppression, and electric discharge. The entire set should feel like sacred lepido-tech myth: colossal scale, dense luminous haze, and wing-driven cosmology.

**Visual Direction:** Age of the Butterfly trading card illustration in the niji 6 anime painterly style, cross-set visual DNA from Glass Absolute plus Blazing Garden. Keep strict readability, bold silhouette, clean focal subject, mythic composition. No mundane terrain, no cities, no modern tech. Use world-atmosphere, suspended debris, geometric chrysalis forms, luminous particulate fog, prismatic refraction, mirror-black obsidian, and elegant sacred symmetry.

**Palette Lock:** obsidian black glass, reflective silver, rust-iron red, oxidized copper teal, pale titanium blue-white, luminous white, electric cyan, ember orange, spectral violet.

**Parameters on every prompt:** `--ar 2:3 --niji 6 --stylize 900`
