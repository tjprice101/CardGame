# Pyroabyss Set — Midjourney Art Prompts

Pyroabyss trading card illustration in the niji 6 anime painterly style. Strictly two-tone palette: deep void purple and blazing crimson red only, with pale ash white highlights — no other colors. Bold ink outlines thick to thin, cel shaded with visible brushwork, painterly and expressive. Characters fill 80% of the frame. Backgrounds are loose expressive dark brushwork. Energy effects are gestural painted flares of crimson and purple. Lighting expressed as: deep void purples in shadow areas, blazing crimsons at energy peaks, pale ash whites on focal points.

Parameters on every prompt: --ar 2:3 --niji 6 --stylize 900
