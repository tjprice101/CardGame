Element Identity: monochrome Vorthum steel, refracted light-memory, storm-borne serpent flight, buried ancestral weight, fracture-road stealth, and prophetic frozen-color omens.
Visual Direction: Prismatic Accord trading card illustration in the niji 6 anime painterly style. Palette of Vorthum graphite, mirror silver, spectrum cyan, fracture violet, accord gold, and white beam-light. Shardborn bodies should read as colorless steel first, then erupt into prismatic shimmer only where light hits. Smooth interlocking scales, fracture-line lattices, white pupil-less eyes, mirror plains, crystal fault roads, and storm ceilings full of spectral bodies. Bold ink outlines thick to thin, cel shaded with visible brushwork, painterly and expressive. Figures fill 80% of the frame. Backgrounds are loose expressive brushwork with reflective steel, mist, storm haze, and fracture-light bloom. YGO secret rare card art meets pokemon illustration rare full art composition.
