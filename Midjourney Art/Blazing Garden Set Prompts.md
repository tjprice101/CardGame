# Blazing Garden Set — Midjourney Art Prompts

**Story Premise:** Blazing Garden takes place on Soleth Vair, a world with no ordinary soil, horizon, or open sky. Life is grown from root-lattices, petal cathedrals, and bloom engines that burn from within rather than being consumed by outside fire. Roses, sunflowers, and thistles function as distinct lineages of sacred biology, each one carrying chromatic flame as blood, memory, and song. When a bloom is cut down it does not die; it is absorbed into the Ember Grove, where its color-memory can be rekindled into new growth. Every lineage converges toward the Final Chord, the world’s total bloom event.

**Visual Direction:** Blazing Garden trading card illustration in the niji 6 anime painterly style. Strict symmetry, recursive floral architecture, and highly readable card compositions. No ordinary terrain, no dirt, no stone, no ruins, no generic forest floor. The world should feel like living botanical divinity: layered roots, translucent petals, seed halos, flame-veined stems, and floral megastructures lit from within. Fire is always internal to plant tissue, never a normal blaze. Palette lock: ember-gold, rose red, sunlit amber, chromatic cyan, spectral violet, deep green, and blackened char accents only where needed for contrast. Figures should feel sacred, ornate, mythic, and precise, with bold ink outlines, luminous gradients, visible brushwork, and a strong trading-card silhouette.

**Parameters on every prompt:** `--ar 2:3 --niji 6 --stylize 900`
