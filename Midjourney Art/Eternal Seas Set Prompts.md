# Eternal Seas Set - Midjourney Art Prompts

**Story Premise:** Veleth Naur is a world-ocean of absolute depth where warm white water and cold black water form continent-scale moving boundaries called Veilmargins. Life is neon from the cellular level, and mythic deep beings reshape currents, pressure, and electromagnetic weather.

**Visual Direction:** Eternal Seas trading card illustration in the niji 6 anime painterly style, aligned in craft and complexity to Glass Absolute and Blazing Garden while remaining distinctly aquatic and bioluminescent. Emphasize volumetric depth layering, white/black current contrast, sacred symmetry, clear card-readability, strong focal silhouette, and painterly light control. No beaches, ships, submarines, modern diving gear, ruins, or terrestrial shoreline motifs.

**Palette Lock:** warm biological white, abyssal black, silver twilight, neon magenta-ultraviolet, toxic neon green, pulse orange, deep red warning tones, yellow-white deep light, purple-blue basslight.

**Parameters on every prompt:** `--ar 2:3 --niji 6 --stylize 900`
