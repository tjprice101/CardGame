# Black Glass Inferno Set - Midjourney Art Prompts (v5 - Lore-Aligned Thematic Differentiation)

**Element Identity:** Colossal draconic war dynasties forged in black-and-white inferno, bound by living black-and-white black-glass roses, covenant scars, and multicolored grief-fire that refracts into controlled chromatic undertones. The clash between Vaelthorax's silver philosophy of inevitable consumption and Morvakael's copper belief in darkness as sustenance. The tragedy of Sorveth's bicolored hope. The garden of roses that grew from dragon blood and the memory of a war that refused to end.
**Visual Direction:** Black Glass Inferno trading card illustration in the niji 6 anime painterly style. Render seraphim as geometric ancient sovereigns?white-silver scales in rigid formations, platinum accents, roses that open spread-petaled to the sky. Render ophanims as copper-traced wanderers?sinuous and searching, moving through shadow, their roses curled inward harboring warmth. Render cherubim as the chromatic collision itself?the merged white-black flames where opposing philosophies consume together. Render angels as immense pivotal beings whose breath changed ages. Keep strong monochrome foundations (white, black, obsidian, alabaster) with chromatic accents isolated to grief-blue, sorrow-violet, copper-rage, and blood-gold fracture lines. Bold ink outlines thick to thin, cel shaded with visible brushwork, painterly and expressive. Subjects fill 80% of frame. Backgrounds: ash courts, ruined cathedrals, war plateaus, veilplane rifts, storming cinder skies?places where things have already burned.
**Parameters on every prompt:** `--ar 2:3 --niji 6 --stylize 900`
