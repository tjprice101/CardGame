# Glass Absolute Set — Midjourney Art Prompts

**Story Premise:** There is no soil, no horizon, no sky in Glass Absolute. Vyrel is a world of living prism-glass where beings are fractals of light taking shape inside an endless medium of transparent geometry. Every creature is a glass-body made of recursive facets, and every gesture is a refraction event. They do not walk on terrain; they move through chambers of interlocked prisms, mirrored planes, suspended shards, and light itself.

**Visual Direction:** Glass Absolute trading card illustration in the niji 6 anime painterly style. Pure glass bodies, recursive fractal anatomy, no organic skin, no dirt, no stone, no plants, no terrain. The world is all crystal planes, suspended prisms, mirrored refraction chambers, and fields of white light splitting into spectral bands. Palette lock: clear glass, white light, pale cyan, spectral silver, soft violet, and deep shadowless midnight only where needed for contrast. Figures should feel translucent, elegant, and impossible, with bold ink outlines thick to thin, visible brushwork, cel shading, and luminous refraction effects. Keep compositions readable and mythic, with card art that feels like a divine optical event.

**Parameters on every prompt:** `--ar 2:3 --niji 6 --stylize 900`
