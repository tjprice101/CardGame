# Infinite Cards Set — Midjourney Art Prompts

**Element Identity:** Transcendence beyond element. Every Infinite card is forged from eternal copies and exists outside the elemental spectrum entirely.
**Visual Direction:** Infinite cards trading card illustration in the niji 6 anime painterly style. Zero-hue monochromatic palette — mirror-chrome silver, polished platinum, and absolute black only. No element colors survive the transcendence; each card carries only the ghost-fossil of its source element's shapes sealed in flawless chrome. Where fire was, there are ember-shaped chrome voids. Where thorns were, there are thorn-fossil impressions under chrome plating. Where light blazed gold, there is now blinding silver-white radiance. Entities are chrome-sealed — no faces visible through sealed helms, no organic surfaces remaining, only crystalline precision lattice and machine-cut geometry. Backgrounds are post-reality void: chrome lattice megastructures, infinite chrome mirror plains, empty silver geometry suspended in absolute black. Energy is cold silver-white light pulses, not warm glow — starlight through a prism compressed to pure white. Compositions are bold, extreme, and uniquely readable. Bold ink outlines thick to thin, cel shaded with visible brushwork, painterly and expressive. Figures fill 80% of the frame. YGO secret rare card art meets pokemon illustration rare full art composition.
**Parameters on every prompt:** `--ar 2:3 --niji 6 --stylize 900`
