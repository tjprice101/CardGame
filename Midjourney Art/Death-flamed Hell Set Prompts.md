# Death-flamed Hell Set — Midjourney Art Prompts

**Story Premise:** Vakhresh is the Ninth Cinder, the floor beneath every hell, a cathedral-realm whose ceiling is the cooled skulls of every demon that ever tried to climb out and whose only weather is deathfire — a flame that burns nothing living, only what has already been extinguished. Deathfire burns in a single sovereign color: a blinding bone-white core licked at every edge by sanguine crimson, as though the last heat of the universe were running on the last drop of blood. The Pale Cohort — the demons of Vakhresh — are made of soot, marrow, and oath-ash. Their flesh is monochrome; their fire is white. Their hierarchy turns on a single regal principle: to burn brightest is to be most thoroughly dead, and the brightest of them all is Khorr-Vael, the No-Face, an emperor-silhouette whose death-flame is now a second sun. Beneath him reign the Seven Ash-Choirs and the bridal processions of Veil-Iorn, the Pale Bridegroom, and the Hollowking Velruth. Every card in the set is a regal, cinematic act of burning — sovereign demons in absolute monochrome, crowned in catastrophic white-and-crimson fire that looks the same on a foot-soldier as it does on a god.

**Visual Direction:** Death-flamed Hell trading card illustration in the niji 6 painterly anime style. Render the Pale Cohort as regal, cinematic, monumental demons in three colors only — char-black silhouettes; bone-white skull, hair, teeth, claw, thorn-crown, and chain accents; ash-gray oilskin draping. Two visual systems, never blended: the body is strictly black, white, and ash-gray, and the death-flame is strictly white and crimson — a searing bone-white core surrounded by deep crimson plasma-veins, vermilion edge-licks, blood-red outermost rim, sanguine sparks, and ash-white caustic. Every card is built at apocalyptic intensity: the figure stands as a dense char-black silhouette backlit and rim-lit by a vast wall of churning crimson plasma-sky that fills the entire upper background with billowing white-cored crimson flame-clouds at maximum saturation. The air is choked with a torrential downpour of crimson embers and white sparks filling every gap of the frame. Long flowing bone-white hair, ash-gray oilskin streamers, and torn fabric tatters are caught and dragged upward by the heat-updraft. Bodies are hand-painted with intricate detail — scaled bone-plate ornament, layered char-black fabric tatters, woven ash-thread embroidery, thorn-crown halos, chain-trailings, spiked pauldrons. Hieratic frontal poses, processional diagonals, sovereign upshots, low-angle monumental framing — even foot-soldiers are framed as world-shaking apparitions. Backgrounds are the screaming crimson plasma-sky of Vakhresh first and foremost, with skull-ceiling vault, ash-spike plains, or cathedral silhouettes only as foreground props beneath the sky-fire. Bold ink outlines thick to thin, cel shaded with visible brushwork, painterly, regal, cinematic, the brightest single object in any hell. Palette lock: char-black, ash-gray, and bone-white for the body; searing bone-white core with crimson, vermilion, blood-red, and ash-white caustic for the death-flame; absolutely no other colors anywhere in the frame.

**Parameters on every prompt:** `--ar 2:3 --niji 6 --stylize 900`
