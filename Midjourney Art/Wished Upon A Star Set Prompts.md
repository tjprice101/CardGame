# Wished Upon A Star — Midjourney Art Prompts

**Story Premise:** In the Wishwright's Age — before the first light cooled — three cosmic beings stood sentinel at the edge of creation. Aethervex, the Wishwright, built his wings from galaxy-thread and swallowed dead suns for breakfast. Selenira kept darkness patient through millennia of ash-white vigilance. Draethos bent time into loops of longing until he forgot which form he'd started in. When the Age ended (not with a collapse, but with a wish unfulfilled), their power scattered as Starlight Charges into every card played in the arena. Now their children walk as playing cards, accumulating Dream Lattice, firing Nova Wish Bursts, and dreaming of a Full-fire Gate that will let them pay back everything the cosmos took.

**Visual Direction:** Wished Upon A Star trading card illustration in the niji 6 painterly anime style — "Painted Cosmic Wrath." A strict two-layer palette: the card body rendered in deep-space monochrome (obsidian-black, ash-charcoal, and silver-white for bone, hair, wing-membrane, and armor accents) and the Stellar Wish energy rendered in pearlescent-neon accents only (pale-gold starlight, ice-blue dreamfire, soft violet nebula-glow, and silver-white nova-burst). No warm amber, no orange, no red fire — the wish-flame is exclusively cool-spectrum: pearl-white at the core, ice-blue plasma-veins, violet-edge drift, and silver-white caustic sparks. Backgrounds: deep-space black sky packed with star-fields, distant dying-star nebulae (pale violet, ice-blue, and silver), and galaxy-ribbon silhouettes. Figures are monumental cosmic beings — winged seraphic colossi, silver-armored war-goddesses, unstable titan-child hybrids, frost-voiced choirs of frozen silver beings. Bold ink outlines thick to thin, cel shaded with visible brushwork, painterly, cinematic, starfield-packed backgrounds.

**Parameters on every prompt:** `--ar 2:3 --niji 6 --stylize 900`
